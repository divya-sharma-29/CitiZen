# CitiZen

## AI-Powered Civic Intelligence Platform

---

# Why use CitiZen?

Unlike traditional civic complaint systems, CitiZen focuses on speed, transparency, community participation, and intelligent decision-making.

### Key Benefits

- ⚡ Report civic issues in under 30 seconds.
- 🤖 AI automatically identifies the issue and estimates its severity.
- 🕵️ Optional anonymous reporting to protect citizen privacy.
- 📍 Real-time tracking of issue progress.
- ❤️ Recognize and appreciate the people who solve civic problems.
- 🌍 Encourage citizens to actively participate in building a cleaner, safer, and smarter city.

# Vision

CitiZen aims to bridge the gap between citizens and local authorities by providing an AI-powered platform for reporting, tracking, and intelligently prioritizing civic issues.

Instead of functioning as a traditional complaint portal, CitiZen transforms citizen reports into actionable insights that help authorities make faster and better decisions.

---


# Problem Statement

Many cities rely on fragmented systems for reporting civic issues such as potholes, garbage accumulation, broken streetlights, water leaks, and damaged public infrastructure.

These systems often suffer from:

- Duplicate complaints
- Poor prioritization
- Slow response times
- Lack of transparency
- Limited communication between citizens and authorities
- Difficulty identifying high-risk localities

As a result, citizens lose confidence in the reporting process while authorities struggle to allocate resources effectively.

---

# Proposed Solution

CitiZen provides a centralized AI-powered platform where citizens can easily report civic issues using photos and location.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence to:

- Identify the issue category from uploaded images.
- Generate issue descriptions.
- Estimate issue severity.
- Detect duplicate reports.
- Highlight localities requiring immediate attention.
- Provide analytics dashboards for authorities.

Citizens can also track the progress of their reports, increasing transparency and accountability.

"Trust Through Verification."

Every important action—reporting an issue, marking it complete, or closing it—should be verifiable rather than relying on a single person's word.
